Pokémon Showdown: https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown
Browser client: https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client
Official online game: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/

Orbit provides an original launcher and friend-battle instructions. No server,
battle engine, client code or Pokémon artwork is copied into this integration.
The official browser client deliberately refuses to initialize in a frame;
Orbit opens it in a separate tab instead of bypassing that restriction.
Battles, teams, accounts and matchmaking are provided by Pokémon Showdown.
