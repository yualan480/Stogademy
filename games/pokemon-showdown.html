Battle a friend

  1. Meet online. Both players open Showdown on their own device or browser and choose a name.
  2. Find your opponent. Use “Find a user” to search for your friend’s Showdown name, then choose “Challenge.”
  3. Choose your battle. Try Random Battle for ready-made teams, or build your own. Your friend accepts to begin.

Looking for an opponent? Use the game’s “Battle!” button for matchmaking.
