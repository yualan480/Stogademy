Battle a friend

You both play right here in Orbit, using separate devices or browsers.

  1. Choose an unused Showdown name. This client supports guest names.
  2. Use Find a user to find your friend, then choose Challenge.
  3. Choose Random Battle for ready-made teams. Your friend accepts to begin.

Use Battle! to find an online opponent instead.
