    Draw & Guess

    One player gets a secret word and has to draw it. Everyone else races to type the right answer before the time runs out. The faster you guess, the more points you score, and everybody takes a turn drawing.

    • 2 to 5 players per room
    • The host can start as soon as 2 players are in
    • Pick colours, brush sizes, fill and undo

    Find players online, or make a room and share the 4-letter code with your friends.

    Archery

    Draw your bow, read the wind and put your arrows in the gold. Everyone shoots at the same time, three arrows per end, and the targets get farther away as the match goes on. Highest score wins.

    • 2 to 5 players per room
    • Hold to draw the bow, let go at full power
    • Wind pushes your arrow, so aim to make up for it

    Find players online, make a room and share the 4-letter code, or practice alone.

    Block Breaker Duel

    Block Breaker for two. You and your rival each get a board with the very same bricks. Break them faster, send junk rows to bury your rival, and keep your own bricks away from your paddle. Last one standing wins, or the higher score when 2 minutes are up.

    • 2 players, side by side, same bricks
    • Every 5 bricks you break sends your rival a junk row
    • Move with the mouse, your finger or the arrow keys

    Find an opponent online, make a room and share the 4-letter code, or play the computer.
