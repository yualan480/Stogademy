BLOCK BREAKERDUEL

Two players, two boards, the very same bricks. Break bricks faster than your rival.

  • Every 5 bricks you break sends a junk row to your rival
  • Clear your whole board to send 2 junk rows at once
  • Bricks reaching your paddle, or no lives left, is a K.O.
  • No K.O.? The higher score after 2 minutes wins
Computer skill

Move with the mouse, your finger or the arrow keys. Space or a tap launches the ball. Online play needs a connection to the matchmaking server.
