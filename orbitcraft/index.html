Inventory controls

Left-click to carry or place a stack. Right-click to split a stack or place one item. Arrange ingredients, then click the output. Shift-click for quick transfer; in hand inventory, Shift-click armor to equip it. Drag left to divide evenly or right to place one per slot.

Make yourself at home.

A shared, persistent block world with PvP enabled. Aim at another player and hold left click (or the mine control on touch) to attack. Empty hands deal 1 HP (half a heart) of base damage; swords deal 4–8 HP. Armor and directional shields reduce damage. Death drops your carried items; use Respawn to return. Everyone online plays in this same world. Build kindly: other people’s creations are part of it.

Inventory / chat / pause menu

Change your controls in Esc → Options. Hold a tool in your right hand to place blocks from your left hand. Beds need two clear spaces on solid ground.

Hold the mine control until the block cracks and breaks. Dropped blocks float and rotate; walk nearby to collect them. Craft logs into planks, sticks and a crafting table. Place the table and right-click it for a 3×3 crafting grid. Click outside the inventory while carrying a stack to drop it; right-click outside to drop just one. Click an inventory stack to carry it, click a grid slot to place it, and right-click to place one. Click the output to craft. Furnaces have input, fuel and output slots. Mine cobblestone with a wooden pickaxe; iron needs stone, gold and diamond need iron, and ancient debris needs diamond. Smelt raw ores and food in a furnace. Three sticks and three strings craft a bow at a crafting table. Keep arrows in your inventory or offhand, hold right-click to draw, and release to shoot. A one-second draw gives full power. Press F while hovering over an inventory item to swap it into your offhand. Flint above a stick above a feather crafts four arrows. Chickens drop feathers; gravel has a 10% chance to drop flint instead of gravel. Coal above a stick crafts four torches; place torches on floors or walls for light. Right-click signs to edit four lines of text. Your first skin choice is free; change it later in Esc → Options → Change Skin for 5,000 Orbits. Explore down to Y=-64, with deepslate, deeper ores and underground caves, or build up to Y=319. Open the recipe book for supported recipes and the full Minecraft reference catalog. Missed arrows can be picked up and reused; arrows that hit an entity are consumed. Tools occupy one item per slot. Six planks in a staircase pattern craft four oak stairs. Six planks in two rows with a stick below the center craft three oak signs. Stairs face your placement direction and can be walked up without jumping. Five planks in a U shape at a crafting table craft an oak boat. Right-click to place and ride it; hold Shift to leave it. Riding a boat prevents fall damage. Three iron ingots in a V craft a bucket; right-click water to scoop it, then right-click a free space to pour it. Use a hoe on grass, then plant seeds on farmland. Crops mature in two minutes, faster near water. Right-click a bed at any time to set your respawn. At night, lie down to sleep. Breaking that bed returns your respawn to the original world spawn. All living players must sleep to skip the night. Hold right-click on food for 1.6 seconds to eat while the eating animation plays. Releasing early cancels it. Equip a shield and hold right-click to block. Double-tap Space toggles flight only when the server has granted creative mode; Space ascends and Shift descends. Right-click water with a fishing rod, wait for a bite, then use again. Water slows you down; hold jump to swim. Bedrock forms the bottom of the world.

This first public world is 1,024 × 1,024 blocks, with forests, meadow, desert, alpine mountains, lakes, and underground caves. Terrain streams in full-height 16 × 16 columns as you explore. Change render distance in Esc → Options. Farms, furnaces and creatures pause beyond the two-chunk simulation distance of every living player. The online count includes only real connected players. Creatures are server-controlled NPCs. The shared world uses Easy survival: fewer night monsters, shorter detection range, gentler damage, and more time to escape creepers. Endermen have a 50% chance to drop one ender pearl. Right-click while holding a pearl to throw it and teleport to where it lands. Endermen are only provoked by looking directly into their eyes. Looking at their body or hitting them does not provoke them. Once provoked, they pursue the player who made eye contact until that player escapes their detection range. Existing hostile mobs burn gradually in daylight. Press T for chat and hold Tab to see everyone online. Colored markers on the locator bar point toward other players, even beyond render distance. Center means ahead; edge arrows mean turn left or right toward someone behind you. Small arrows mark players above or below you. Match marker colors to names in the Tab player list.
