Inventory controls

Left-click to carry or place a stack. Right-click to split a stack or place one item. Arrange ingredients, then click the output. Shift-click for quick transfer; in hand inventory, Shift-click armor to equip it. Drag left to divide evenly or right to place one per slot.

Inventory and builds save automatically.

Make yourself at home.

A shared, persistent block world with PvP enabled. Aim at another player and hold left click (or the mine control on touch) to attack. Empty hands deal 1 HP (half a heart) of base damage; swords deal 4–8 HP. Armor and directional shields reduce damage. Death drops your carried items; use Respawn to return. Everyone online plays in this same world. Build kindly: other people’s creations are part of it.

Inventory / chat / pause menu

Change your controls in Esc → Options. Hold a tool in your right hand to place blocks from your left hand. Beds need two clear spaces on solid ground.

Hold the mine control until the block cracks and breaks. Dropped blocks float and rotate; walk nearby to collect them. Craft logs into planks, sticks and a crafting table. Place the table and right-click it for a 3×3 crafting grid. Click an inventory stack to carry it, click a grid slot to place it, and right-click to place one. Click the output to craft. Furnaces have input, fuel and output slots. Mine cobblestone with a wooden pickaxe; iron needs stone, gold and diamond need iron, and ancient debris needs diamond. Smelt raw ores and food in a furnace. Coal above a stick crafts four torches; place torches on solid ground for light. Five planks in a U shape at a crafting table craft an oak boat. Use a hoe on grass, then plant seeds on farmland. Crops mature in two minutes, faster near water. Right-click beds at night to sleep and set your respawn. All living players must sleep to skip the night. Hold right-click on food for 1.6 seconds to eat while the eating animation plays. Releasing early cancels it. Equip a shield and hold right-click to block. Double-tap Space toggles flight only when the server has granted creative mode; Space ascends and Shift descends. Right-click water with a fishing rod, wait for a bite, then use again. Water slows you down; hold jump to swim. Bedrock forms the bottom of the world.

This first public world is 1,024 × 1,024 blocks, with forests, meadow, desert, alpine mountains, lakes, and underground caves. Terrain streams in full-height 16 × 16 columns as you explore. Change render distance in Esc → Options. Farms, furnaces and creatures pause beyond the two-chunk simulation distance of every living player. The online count includes only real connected players. Creatures are server-controlled NPCs.
